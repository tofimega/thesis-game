
## Bomberman (DOS)
- Similar goal: defeat all enemies
- Player has a similar move set: 
	- Movement up-down, left-right
	- Creating and placing bombs
- Differences:
	- Faster pace
	- Core gameplay loop is different:
		- Instead of uncovering secrets from under blocks, the focus is entirely on the enemies
		- Enemies have more complex behavior The goal is not to trap them in a corner, but to exploit this behavior
