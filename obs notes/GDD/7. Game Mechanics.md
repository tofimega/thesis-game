# Player states
- Default:
	- Walking
		- Moves at a relatively decent pace
		- (Diagonals?)
	- Standing
		- Standing
	- Hit
		- Knockback ~ 1.5 tiles
		- Player input is ignored during this (~ .5 s)
		- Bomb is dropped (thrown with less force)
		- 1-2 s Invincibility after
	- Creating bomb
		- Player input is ignored during this (~ .5 s)
		- Bomb spawns attached to player
		- Player will switch to Carrying mode
- Carrying:
	- Walking
		- Happens at a slightly slower speed
		- Risk - Reward: Harder to dodge enemies - Ready to fight back
	- Standing
	- Hit
		- Bomb is dropped at player's position
	- Throwing Bomb
		- Player input is ignored during this (~ 1 s)
		- Bomb is thrown forward 3-4 tiles
		- Bomb cannot explode while it is being thrown
			- Risk - Reward:
				- Risk: Waiting for the fuse to almost go out while carrying -> risk of explosion
				- Reward: Bomb will explode right when it lands -> more precise timing / aim
# Bomb mechanics
- Spawns attached to player
- Has a fuse length of ~ 1.5-3 s
- Indicator when very short time left ~ .3 s
- Cannot explode while being thrown

- Explosion engulfs a circle w a radius of ~ 2-3 tiles
- Explosion lasts ~ 1 s
# General enemy mechanics
- An enemy can only be hurt if it's in a specific state
- The enemy switches states based on the player's actions
- ***Think about RISK and REWARD!*** The enemy becoming vulnerable should also put the player in danger somehow
# Level mechanics

- One level consist of 10-15 screens
- One screen is a ~ 16 x 16 Tilemap
- It can contain static tiles (floors, walls, etc.) and dynamic objects (anything with behavior)
- Some screens (or tiles) may have special properties that modify the behavior of certain objects
	- Examples:
		- slippery tiles will cause the player to maintain a great deal of inertia during movement
		- The bomb's fuse can't be lit in damp rooms the player will instead have to use the enemies to cause the bomb to explode
- Once all enemies have been cleared, the game moves on to the next screen
- When all screens are cleared, the level is completed, and the next level unlocks
# Game progression
- The game consists of ~ 5-10 levels
- The levels are unlocked in a linear order
- Certain levels may also reward the player with permanent upgrades
	- Following levels will require the player to make use of these
- When a level is completed, a brief results screen is shown, during which progress is saved, and the next level is loaded
	- Transition from one level to the results screen to the next level is done seamlessly, this means the results screen must be embedded into either the game scene or the global UI
