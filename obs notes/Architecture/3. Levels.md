

- One screen is a 16 \* 16 tilemap onto which static tiles (walls, floor, etc.) and spawn points for dynamic objects (anything with behavior) are placed
- When a level loads all screens are loaded, but dynamic objects are not yet instantiated
- When a screen is initialized, all spawn points on the screen spawn their objects, the spawn points are unloaded
- After which the player character is initialized and placed at the beginning point of the screen
- After a screen is cleared, all remaining dynamic objects are unloaded, the camera moves to the next screen, and the process repeats
- The player exists separately from the levels
