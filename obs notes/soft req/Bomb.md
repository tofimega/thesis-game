
Modes
- Inactive:
	- no collision
	- no timers
	- invisible
	- out of tree
- Spawned (Carry):
	- No collison
	- conneted to player's remoteTransform
	- Timer ticks
	- can explode
- Thrown:
	- No collision (detects walls)
	- move x units (or until wall)
	- Timer ticks
	- cannot explode (wait until grounded)
- Grounded:
	- Collision
	- Timer ticks
	- Can explode
Fuse state:
- Unlit:
	- nothing
	- invisible
- Lit:
	- animation: default
- Short:
	- animation: flash
- Explode: 
	- spawn explosion
	- invisble
